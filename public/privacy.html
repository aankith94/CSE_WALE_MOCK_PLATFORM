Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what CSE WALE ("we") collects through https://csewalelivemockplatform.vercel.app/ and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Name, email address and mobile number, entered by you or received from Google when you choose Google login.
  • Your test attempts: answers, scores, time spent and ranks.
  • Payment status and the Razorpay payment and order IDs. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay handles them.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To create your account, unlock your tests, and show your result and rank.
  • To confirm payments and answer your support requests.
  • To improve the tests and the site.
  • To contact you about your account or new tests. We do not send promotions without a way to opt out.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is processed only by the services that run the site: Supabase (database and login), Vercel (hosting), Razorpay (payments) and Google (if you use Google login). We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's storage only to keep you logged in and to remember your test progress and preferences. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data by writing to aankith2003@gmail.com. Payment records may be kept as long as the law requires.

Security

Data is stored with access controls and sent over HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.
