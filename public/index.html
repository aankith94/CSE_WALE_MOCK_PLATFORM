Yes. Mock Test 1 is free for every logged-in student, so you can try the exam interface, the paper, the solutions and the report card before you pay. The other tests unlock with the ₹99 pass.

Is ₹99 one-time?

Yes. One payment unlocks every test in the series, including the ones added later.

Can I pause the test?

No. Like the real exam, the timer runs continuously and is kept by our server. If your phone or browser closes, your answers are saved and you can come back and resume until the time runs out.

Your first attempt is the ranked one. After that you can practise again as often as you like: the full test, only the wrong questions, or the wrong plus unattempted ones. Practice attempts do not change your rank.
